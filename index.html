Delta, Nigeria

Delta State is a state in southern Nigeria, located in the Niger Delta region, with Asaba as its capital and Warri as one of its major commercial cities. It is bordered by Edo State to the north, Anambra State to the east, Rivers State and Bayelsa State to the south/east, and Ondo State to the northwest, while its southern boundary extends to the Atlantic Ocean. The state is known for its rich oil and gas resources, diverse ethnic groups, fertile lands, rivers, and vibrant commercial activities.
